About ELTAN

Empowering English Language Teachers Across Nigeria

ELTAN (English Language Teachers Association of Nigeria) is dedicated to fostering excellence in English language teaching and learning. Join a vibrant community of educators committed to professional growth and educational excellence.

  • Annual National Conference & Workshop with expert speakers
  • Professional development programs and CPD opportunities
  • Special Interest Groups focused on teaching methodologies
  • FREE PRELIM resources for teaching Junior Secondary students
  • Networking with English language professionals nationwide

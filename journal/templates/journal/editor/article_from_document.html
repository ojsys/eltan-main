What is read out of a Word file

TitleFrom the document's title style or its first line
AuthorsFrom the byline under the title, when it is one
AbstractFrom the Abstract heading to Keywords or the introduction
KeywordsFrom the keywords line
SectionsHeadings, paragraphs, lists, tables, quotes and figures
ReferencesSet as a hanging-indent list

Where it needs your eye

A manuscript is laid out for a human reader, not for a parser. Headings written in Word's heading styles, in bold, or numbered are all found; one that is none of those is read as an ordinary paragraph. The next screen lists every section that was detected, which is the quickest way to see whether the document was understood.

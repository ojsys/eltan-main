What happens next

  1. Each file is read for its title, byline, abstract and keywords.
  2. One staged article is created per file — none of them public.
  3. You get a screen listing all of them, with what was found in each, to correct where the reading went wrong.
  4. Tick the ones to publish. Anything you leave unticked stays staged until you come back to it.

On reading the files

A paper is laid out for a human eye, so what comes back is a good guess and no more — a scanned PDF with no text layer yields nothing but its filename. Nothing here is published on the strength of that guess: an article with no byline cannot be ticked to publish at all.

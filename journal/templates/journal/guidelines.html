What you will need to submit

1. An anonymised manuscript. Review is double-blind, so this file must not name you: remove author names and affiliations, acknowledgements, funding details that identify you, and any self-citation phrased as "as we showed in…".

2. A separate title page. Title, every author, their affiliations, and which one is the corresponding author. Reviewers never see this file.

3. An abstract of 50–250 words and 3–6 keywords.

4. Declarations. That the work is original and not under review elsewhere, any competing interests, funding, and ethical approval where people took part in the research.

5. Supplementary material such as instruments or data, if you have any.
